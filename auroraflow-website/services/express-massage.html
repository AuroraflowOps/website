30 Minute Express Massage

$75 30 min

Short on time? Maybe you're new to massage therapy? Then the 30 Minute Express Massage is perfect for you! During this massage, you'll only remove your shoes (all other clothing stays on!), and your massage therapist will focus their work on your head, scalp, and feet while you lie on the massage table.

This massage is perfect for folx who may want to squeeze in some self-care on their work breaks, for folx who may not be comfortable with fully undressing during a massage, for those who are new to massage, and/or for those who want to try massage before committing to a full hour.

What To Expect

During Your Session

  • Shoes off — all other clothing stays on
  • Comfortable, reclined position on the massage table
  • Focused work on the head, scalp and feet
  • A short, relaxing reset — no need to undress or get on the table under a sheet

How To Prepare

  • Wear comfortable clothing you can relax in
  • Clean feet appreciated
  • Mention any areas you'd like extra focus on
  • Great to book on a lunch or work break
