Policy

New: Dementia & Cognitive Conditions Policy

To protect client safety, practitioner safety, and ensure clear consent practices, we've added a new policy for clients with dementia, Alzheimer's, or other cognitive conditions.

  • A caregiver must be present in the treatment room or on-site for the full session
  • The caregiver is required to sign consent forms on behalf of the client
