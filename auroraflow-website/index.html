Queerly Owned & Operated

They say wellness is a luxury.We believe it's a human right.

Auroraflow was built on the premise that everyone deserves a safe, affirming space for self-care.

Based in Downtown Durham, our staff is dedicated to deep, compassionate client care while striving to support our broader community through events, partnerships, and socially conscious initiatives. We do our best to address financial barriers via our Community Fund and our Pay What You Can Days, as well as memberships for both individuals and businesses.
