Skincare & Waxing

BIPOC Specific Clinical Facial & Consultation

$210 105 min

This is more than just a facial. It is the start of a journey to discovering your healthiest and happiest skin. This comprehensive consultation gives you and your esthetician dedicated time to talk through your current routine, goals, health history, and more. Together you will build a treatment plan tailored specifically to your skin.

If you are looking to book a chemical peel, you must book this service first. This facial is designed as a one-and-done starting point. Once you have had the comprehensive facial, please book the service your esthetician recommends.

Keep in mind: Do not exfoliate at home for 2 days before or after your facial.

Estheticians trained and certified by Joelle Lee: Nik Beech-Moore and Christine Link.

Book Appointment Not sure? Book a free consultation

Good For

  • Skin of color
  • Hyperpigmentation & dark spots
  • Post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation
  • Uneven skin tone
  • Pre-peel consultation for BIPOC clients

What to Expect

  • In-depth consultation for skin of color concerns
  • Treatment with products formulated for melanin-rich skin
  • Personalized plan addressing hyperpigmentation & tone
  • Prerequisite for advanced corrective treatments

How to Prepare

  • List current products & past reactions
  • Mention any history with specific skin concerns
  • Come ready to discuss your skin goals openly
  • Note any current medications
