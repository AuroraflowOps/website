Skincare & Waxing

Chemical Peels

From $195 90 min

Chemical peels are a highly effective way to reveal smoother, brighter skin by gently exfoliating the outer layers and promoting cell turnover. While the name sounds intense, our estheticians take a slow and careful approach that makes peels appropriate for all skin types, including skin of color, which has historically been harmed by overly aggressive treatments.

Our estheticians have been specifically trained to administer chemical peels to skin of color by world-renowned esthetician educator Joelle Lee. To achieve the best results, peels are done in a series over time, allowing the skin to gradually improve in texture, tone, and radiance. Prepping your skin with the right products beforehand is essential for safe and effective treatment.

If you are interested in chemical peels, start with a consultation and a Clinical Facial. This allows us to assess your skin and recommend the right peel for your goals. We do not offer one-off chemical peels in order to protect the safety of your skin.

Good For

  • Uneven skin tone & hyperpigmentation
  • Fine lines & texture
  • Acne & post-acne scars
  • Dull, rough skin
  • All skin tones including skin of color

What to Expect

  • Clinical consultation before first peel
  • Chemical solution applied & neutralized
  • Mild tingling or warmth during treatment
  • Possible light peeling in following days

How to Prepare

  • Clinical Facial consultation required first
  • Discontinue retinoids 7+ days before
  • No waxing or laser treatments 2 weeks before
  • Commit to daily SPF 30+ after treatment
