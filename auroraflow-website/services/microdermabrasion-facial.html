Microdermabrasion Facial

$185 60 min

Glow up without intense chemical exfoliation. Our Microdermabrasion Facial uses a diamond-tipped wand to gently exfoliate the surface of the skin while simultaneously vacuuming away debris, revealing smoother and healthier skin underneath.

This facial includes a double cleanse, microdermabrasion exfoliation, extractions, facial massage, mask, shoulder or face massage, serum, moisturizer, and SPF. The suction function stimulates circulation and cell renewal, encouraging collagen production and long-term skin rejuvenation.

This treatment is great for improving skin texture and tone, reducing the appearance of fine lines and mild acne scars, unclogging pores, and enhancing your skin's natural radiance. If you are ever unsure which facial to book, simply choose one and your esthetician will help guide you during the skin analysis.

Also available: Mini Microdermabrasion Facial.

What To Expect

During Your Session

  • Diamond-tip device passed over skin in multiple directions
  • Light suction sensation throughout
  • Mild redness immediately after (fades quickly)
  • Smooth, polished feel right after treatment

How To Prepare

  • Avoid retinoids & exfoliants 5–7 days before
  • No active breakouts or open sores in treatment area
  • Mention any skin conditions or sensitivities
  • Use SPF daily before & after treatment
