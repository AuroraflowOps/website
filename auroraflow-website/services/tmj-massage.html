Massage & Bodywork

TMJ Tune-up

$90 ($80 first session) 30 min

TMJ massage focuses on relieving tension and pain in the temporomandibular joint, which connects the jawbone to the skull and controls jaw movement. When the TMJ is stressed or injured, it can cause discomfort, pain, and difficulty eating, speaking, or even breathing.

Our therapists use gentle but effective techniques to work the muscles surrounding the TMJ, helping to reduce pain and restore mobility. Sessions are approximately 30 minutes. You can also add on a 60 minute Ashiatsu massage for full-body relief.

New clients: book the 30 Minute TMJ Massage (First Time) for your first session at the discounted rate.

Benefits

  • Relieves pain and tension in the jaw, face, neck, and shoulders
  • Reduces headaches and migraines
  • Improves jaw mobility and flexibility
  • Eases clicking or popping in the jaw
  • Reduces stress and promotes relaxation
Book Appointment Not sure? Book a free consultation

Good For

  • Jaw clenching & grinding (bruxism)
  • TMJ disorders
  • Tension headaches
  • Neck & shoulder tension
  • Post-dental procedure discomfort

What to Expect

  • Intraoral (inside the mouth) work possible with consent
  • Focus on jaw, face, head, and neck muscles
  • Pressure relief that can feel intense but deeply satisfying
  • Significant relief often felt in first session

How to Prepare

  • Mention any dental appliances
  • Inform therapist of any jaw surgeries
  • First session is $70 (mention at booking)
  • Schedule regular sessions for best results
