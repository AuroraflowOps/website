Skincare & Waxing

Acne Focused Facial

$145 75 min

Whether you have dealt with acne for years or recently developed it, this facial focuses on clearing your pores and restoring your skin to health. A blueberry enzyme with glycolic and salicylic acids deeply exfoliates and softens debris in the pores. Your esthetician will spend extra time on extractions, clearing blackheads, whiteheads, and milia. High-frequency technology creates an oxygen-rich environment to help kill bacteria and prevent breakouts. A charcoal mask is then applied to clarify and soothe the skin. As with all our facials, you will enjoy a shoulder massage and aromatherapy while your mask sets.

About the Products

Blueberry Enzyme: A powerful antioxidant that brightens the skin using small percentages of glycolic and salicylic acids to digest dead skin cells and soften sebum. The antioxidants in blueberries make this an excellent anti-aging treatment that creates a healthy glow.

Charcoal Mask: Formulated with anti-microbial, skin detoxifying, and pore cleansing ingredients that clarify and purify the skin while absorbing excess oil and impurities. Pore size is visibly reduced and skin is left clear and healthy.

Good For

  • Active breakouts
  • Clogged pores & blackheads
  • Oily & combination skin

What to Expect

  • Detailed skin + lifestyle consultation
  • Deep cleanse & steam
  • Manual extractions

How to Prepare

  • Do not pick or squeeze beforehand
  • Mention current acne medications (especially Accutane)
  • Avoid retinoids 5+ days before
  • Bring a list of current skincare products
