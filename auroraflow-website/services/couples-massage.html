Massage & Bodywork

Couples Massage

From $250 60 min (both people)

Enjoy the benefits of therapeutic massage side-by-side with anyone you'd like to share the experience with: a partner, friend, family member, or chosen person. Both guests receive a full, individually tailored session simultaneously in the same room. A beautiful way to connect, unwind together, and invest in shared wellness.

A 50% deposit is required at the time of booking. If you cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours in advance, your full deposit will be refunded. Cancellations between 4 and 24 hours before your appointment will result in a non-refundable deposit. Cancellations less than 4 hours before the appointment will be charged 100% of the service cost.

Our Couples Massage is open to any two people. Whether you are bringing a friend, partner, family member, or anyone you trust, our specially designed rooms allow two people to enjoy a side-by-side pampering session.

Our therapists use a combination of deep tissue and therapeutic techniques, with each person able to request specific areas of focus or choose their preferred massage style.
