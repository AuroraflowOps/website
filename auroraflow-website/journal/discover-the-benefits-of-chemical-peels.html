Discover the Benefits of Chemical Peels

We're thrilled that our estheticians have received advanced training with world-renowned educator Joelle Lee, equipping them to safely administer chemical peels to skin of every shade.

Auroraflow Team · 2 min read

We're thrilled that a few of our estheticians have received advanced training with the world-renowned educator Joelle Lee. This specialized training equips them with the knowledge and skills to safely and effectively administer chemical peels to skin of color and in turn, skin of every shade. We understand that many people, especially those with melanated skin, may feel apprehensive about chemical peels due to negative experiences or stories online. However, when performed correctly, chemical peels can be transformative for both corrective and preventative skincare!

The Power of Chemical Peels

Chemical peels are a versatile treatment that can address a variety of skin concerns. Some of the most common issues that chemical peels can help with include:

Hyperpigmentation, Dark spots and uneven skin tone can be significantly reduced.
Acne, Chemical peels can help clear up acne and prevent future breakouts.
Scarring, The appearance of acne scars and other types of scarring can be minimized.
Retexturizing, Smooth out rough skin texture for a more refined look.
Aging, Promote youthful, radiant skin by reducing fine lines and wrinkles.

Safety First: Treating Skin Gently and in Stages

One of the keys to successful chemical peels for melanated skin is to approach treatment gently and in stages. Our estheticians are trained to evaluate your skin and customize the treatment plan accordingly. This careful approach helps to prevent common issues like hyperpigmentation or scarring, ensuring that your skin heals beautifully and safely.

If you're curious about chemical peels and want to explore how they can benefit your skin, we recommend booking a Clinical Facial and Consultation. During this appointment, our estheticians will assess your skin type, discuss your skincare goals, and create a personalized treatment plan.

We're passionate about helping our clients achieve their best skin! If you've been hesitant to try chemical peels, now is the perfect time to see what a difference professional, knowledgeable care can make.

Book your Clinical Facial and Consultation today and start your journey to radiant, healthy skin.
