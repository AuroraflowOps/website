Scar Tissue Massage

From $65 30 min

Scar tissue massage is a specialized technique that improves the function and sensation of tissue around post-surgical or traumatic scars. By encouraging better movement between the skin, fascia, and underlying muscle layers, this work can reduce tightness, numbness, and restricted mobility, and support both physical and emotional healing around the scar site.

Book Appointment Not sure? Book a free consultation

Good For

  • Post-surgical scars (C-section, joint surgery, etc.)
  • Traumatic injury scars
  • Restricted mobility around scar sites
  • Numbness or altered sensation
  • Emotional processing around scars

What to Expect

  • Focused work on and around the scar
  • Gentle but specific pressure
  • Discussion of scar history and goals
  • Can be combined with therapeutic massage

How to Prepare

  • Scar must be fully closed, no open wounds
  • Ideally wait 6–8 weeks post-surgery
  • Mention how old the scar is
  • Wear clothing that allows easy access to the area
