Massage & Bodywork

Hot Stone Massage

From $145 60 min

Smooth, heated stones are placed along the body and used as an extension of the therapist’s hands. The warmth softens muscle tissue before deeper work begins, so tension releases with less pressure and far less bracing than a purely hands-on session.

It is a good choice for chronic tightness, cold-weather stiffness, and anyone who wants deep relief without deep discomfort. The heat also does quiet work on circulation and on a nervous system that has been running hot.

Stone temperature is checked with you throughout, and can be adjusted or set aside at any point in the session.

FAQ

Common questions

How hot are the stones?
Warm, not uncomfortable. Your therapist tests every stone before it touches you and checks in with you during the session. If anything feels too warm, say so — the temperature is adjusted or the stones set aside.
How is this different from a regular massage?
The heat does part of the work. Warm stones soften muscle tissue first, so tension releases at lighter pressure. Clients who find deep tissue too intense often get the same relief from a hot stone session.
How long is a session?
Hot Stone Massage sessions start at 60 minutes.
Is there anyone who should skip hot stone?
Heat is not appropriate for everyone. Let us know if you are pregnant, have neuropathy, diabetes, high blood pressure, recent injuries or inflammation, or any condition affecting heat sensitivity, and we will recommend the right service for you.
