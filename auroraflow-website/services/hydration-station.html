Skincare & Waxing

Hydration Station

$135 60 min

Great for all skin types, this water-rich facial helps your skin hold onto moisture through any season. Your esthetician will tailor the treatment to your skin, choosing the right cleanser and exfoliant for you. The star of the service is a cooling peel-off mask that helps prevent trans-epidermal water loss, one of the main causes of dehydrated skin. Paired with a hydrating serum, it is like a weighted blanket for your face, eyes and lips included. As with all our facials, this service includes a skin analysis, extractions, and aromatherapy during a shoulder massage.

Good For

How to Prepare

  • Mention current moisturizers you use
  • Avoid heavy exfoliation the week before
  • Come ready to glow!
