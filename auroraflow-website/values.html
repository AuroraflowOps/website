Who we are

A mission-driven small business, with our values intact.

Auroraflow is queer-owned and rooted in Durham, NC on Lumbee land. We operate as a mission-driven small business that strives to take into account people, planet, and profit.

This means that though we operate as a for-profit entity, Auroraflow was founded on key guiding principles and a spirit of intention to create a space that balances the prosperity of our team, the safety and accessibility of our clients, and the growing needs of our community while minimizing our impact on the environment.

While we strive to do our best to serve all parties, running a business with your values intact isn't easy, especially in today's political climate. So we want to be upfront about the choices we make, why we make them, and where we're still learning and growing.

We don't have all the answers, and we won't get every challenge right. Instead, we want to hold space for gratitude and feedback from our community while also asking for your understanding that often we must make difficult decisions to ensure the sustainability of our business.

Our Commitments

At Auroraflow, we believe wellness is a human right.

For far too long, health and wellness have been treated as a privilege for people who can afford it, shaped by narrow ideas of who the industry decided is worthy of care.

We reject that, and we try to challenge it one business decision at a time.

We hold that care should be accessible, affirming, and rooted in dignity. We also know we can't push back on wellness systems built on racism, classism, and exploitation while recreating those same dynamics inside our own walls.

That means the way we care for people has to include a deep commitment to the people providing that care, so they are also treated with care and dignity. We're committed to building a business where clients and providers alike are valued, respected, and supported.

Economic Accessibility

Taking down the economic walls around care.

The nature of our healthcare and wellness industry has built not only mental walls, but economic walls around care. Too often, the people who need rest and healing most are also the ones who can least afford it. While our small business must maintain a model that cannot fully eliminate these barriers, we do our best to address them through a series of programs and initiatives.

Supporting Artists

Art lives on our walls.

As evidenced by the walls of our treatment rooms, art has always been part of the spirit inside Auroraflow. Before opening, we hired local muralists to bring our rooms to life and create eight unique rooms that give our clients an immersive experience.

  • ✦Commission-free walls. Today, local artists can hang and sell their work in our space commission-free.
  • ✦NewSocial Media Residency. Launched in October 2026, a paid opportunity for a local artist to collaborate with us on a shared piece and get their work in front of our online community.

Voting with Our Dollars

Small and local, wherever we can.

Like most small businesses, we rely on large corporations to reach customers and keep the lights on, but not all of them share our values. So, where we can, we choose small and local instead. That goes for the materials we buy, the services we hire, and the products on our retail shelves.

We'll be honest that sometimes cost has to drive our decisions. Keeping our costs balanced is what lets us pay our team well and keep giving back, but balance is key because we want to grow our business in a way we're proud of.

Responsible Use of AI

A tool, used with intention.

As a small business, we always have to find ways to make the most of every dollar and every hour of our team's time. Generative AI tools, including large language models like ChatGPT and Claude, have opened up real opportunities to improve how we operate as a small, scrappy team. In fact, generative AI is baked directly into almost every industry software, and we are aware it's behind many of the new features our technology vendors are building.

A note from our owner

Deciding how Auroraflow uses AI hasn't been simple. Running my businesses, I work with this technology every day, and I've seen how much it can help a small team. I believe used with intention, it can even help us connect more as humans. But I also know what it costs: our planet, creative work, and the people whose jobs it can change overnight.

Here's where I've landed (as of the time of writing this)

I see AI as a tool. And like any tool, it is only as good as the choices behind it. Behind the scenes our management team uses tools that sometimes leverage generative AI for project management, keeping our website up to date, reporting, accounting, and some editing on our marketing. It takes admin work off our team's plates so they can put more of themselves into caring for you.

What is most important is we don't and won't use it to replace human connection, which is the foundation of everything this business stands for.

Lauren McCulloughOwner, Auroraflow

Here's what that looks like in practice:

01

Your care uses human judgment.

Your provider is fully responsible for your care. Every recommendation comes from their own training and judgment, never from software alone.

02

You'll interact with people, not bots.

When you call, text, or walk through our door, you're talking to a real person on our team. We don't use AI chatbots or automated receptionists.

03

People still own all decisions.

AI tools can help us pull a report or find information faster, but real people make the decisions about Auroraflow and its growth.

04

Your privacy matters.

Whenever we can, we choose software that meets HIPAA standards, even though we aren't legally required to. We turn off AI training settings wherever our tools allow, so your information isn't used to train AI models. We do our best to keep your information confidential, especially the private health details you share with your provider.

05

Our content comes from people.

Much of AI was built on writers' and artists' work without their permission or pay, and we don't want to contribute to that. Everything we share comes from our team, our clients, or the local artists we pay to create with us.

06

A person stands behind it.

Whenever we use AI, someone on our team is responsible for the result. If something goes wrong, we'll own it, fix it, and let you know what we changed.

✦

We'll keep checking in.

We review how we use AI regularly, and if there are significant updates to our guidelines, we will provide transparency by updating them here. Our team can raise concerns anytime. If you ever want to know more, email us at info@auroraflow.com and we'll tell you the truth.
