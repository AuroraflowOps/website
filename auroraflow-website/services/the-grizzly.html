The Grizzly (Beard Facial)

$135 60 min

People with beards need facials too. The Grizzly is a luxurious grooming experience designed to nourish and revitalize both your skin and facial hair. A high-frequency comb clears bacteria, stimulates hair growth, and prevents breakage, resulting in a stronger and smoother beard. The treatment finishes with a deep conditioning oil concentrate to leave the beard soft, shiny, and refreshed.

Like all our facials, this treatment includes a skin analysis, double cleanse, exfoliation, extractions if needed, mask, shoulder and scalp massage with aromatherapy, and serum and moisturizer to close.

  • Mention any skin conditions beneath the beard
