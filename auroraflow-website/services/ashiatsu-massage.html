Ashiatsu "Barefoot" Massage

From $145 60 or 90 min

This unique technique uses the therapist's feet to deliver deep tissue compression massage while clients lie on a traditional massage table. Using overhead bars for balance, the therapist can vary the weight and pressure applied to target deep muscle tension and provide relief from pain and discomfort.

Benefits of Ashiatsu Massage

  • Deep tissue pressure that reaches the deepest layers of muscle tissue, releasing knots and tightness
  • Relaxation and stress relief through long, flowing strokes
  • Improved circulation by increasing the flow of oxygen and nutrients to your muscles
  • Increased flexibility by targeting tight muscles and releasing tension

During the session, the therapist uses overhead bars to control the pressure they apply. This technique is ideal for anyone seeking relief from chronic tension, back spasms, or pinched nerves.
