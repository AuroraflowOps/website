Skincare & Waxing

Spa Facial

$125 60 min

This facial is designed with everyone in mind. Whether your skin is oily, dry, or you are simply curious about facials for the first time, this is a great place to start. Your esthetician will analyze your skin and discuss what approach is best for you. Any facial can be customized during your consultation.

Keep in mind: Do not exfoliate at home for 2 days before or after your facial.

Book Appointment Not sure? Book a free consultation

Good For

  • All skin types
  • Introductory facial clients
  • General skin maintenance
  • Dull or congested skin
  • Stress relief & relaxation

What to Expect

  • Skin analysis & consultation
  • Cleanse, exfoliate, extract, mask & moisturize
  • Shoulder massage included
  • Products from Hale & Hush, dermatologist-tested

How to Prepare

  • Arrive with a clean face if possible
  • Mention any known allergies or sensitivities
  • Avoid exfoliating at home 3–5 days before
  • Remove contacts if worn
