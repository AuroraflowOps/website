Massage & Bodywork

Reflexology

From $85 30 or 45 min

Reflexology is a therapeutic foot massage that applies pressure to specific zones in the feet to promote relaxation and ease tension throughout the body. This practice stimulates the nervous system, encouraging the production of endorphins and other feel-good chemicals in the brain.

Reflexology is an excellent option for those who spend a lot of time on their feet, prefer a gentler form of bodywork, or have physical or mental health conditions that make traditional massage difficult. It is also a welcoming choice for clients managing PTSD, anxiety, or body dysmorphia.

Timing: A 90 minute massage with reflexology includes 30 minutes of reflexology. Reflexology-only appointments are 30 minutes.

What To Expect

During Your Session

  • Focused work on the feet (and sometimes hands)

  • Deeply relaxing, many clients fall asleep

How To Prepare

  • Clean feet appreciated
  • Wear comfortable clothing
  • Mention any foot injuries or conditions
  • Stay hydrated before & after

FAQ

Common questions

How long is a session?
Reflexology-only appointments are 30 minutes. A 90 minute massage with reflexology includes 30 minutes of reflexology.
Who is reflexology a good fit for?
It's an excellent option for those who spend a lot of time on their feet, prefer a gentler form of bodywork, or have physical or mental health conditions that make traditional massage difficult. It is also a welcoming choice for clients managing PTSD, anxiety, or body dysmorphia.
Do I need to prepare anything?
Clean feet are appreciated. Wear comfortable clothing, mention any foot injuries or conditions, and stay hydrated before and after your session.
What are the benefits?
Improves circulation, reduces stress and promotes relaxation, improves sleep quality, reduces foot pain and discomfort, and eases discomfort in the knees and lower back.
