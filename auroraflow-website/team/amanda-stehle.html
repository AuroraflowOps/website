Amanda (LMBT #21770) is a Texas native who now calls North Carolina home. She has been a practicing massage therapist since 2022. With a diverse background in various spas in Texas, Amanda has developed the skills to calm and relax the nervous system. Many people come up from her table feeling rested and refreshed.

Amanda is also an Energy Healer with her own style of energy work that promotes harmonizing the mind body and spirt while providing you a reading on your current energy state. You can enjoy the healing power of energy work blended with massage (energetic bodywork) or go on a guided journey to heal yourself (guided energy healing). Whether you seek relief from tight muscles or desire a transformative healing experience, Amanda invites you to enjoy a massage that leaves you feeling rejuvenated.
