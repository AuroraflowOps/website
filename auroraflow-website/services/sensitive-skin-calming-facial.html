Skincare & Waxing

Sensitive Skin Calming Facial

$145 60 min

Rest assured your skin is in excellent hands with our licensed estheticians. Depending on your skin's needs, your facial will include a skin analysis, double cleanse, customized exfoliation, facial massage, extractions, mask, shoulder massage, and the appropriate serum and moisturizer to finish.

Feel free to bring questions about your at-home skincare routine and let your esthetician build a protocol just for you.

We use Hale and Hush skincare, a line designed for health-challenged skin types including those undergoing chemotherapy. Their products are paraben-free, gluten-free, sulfate-free, dermatologist-tested, and free from artificial fragrances.
