Body & Facial Hair Removal

Pricing varies by service Varies

At Auroraflow, we feel passionately that spa services should help each person feel comfortable in their skin, regardless of size, shape, or gender, which is why our waxing menu has been tailored so that services are based on the time and effort of the esthetician, not the gender or body type of our clients. We are using both hard and soft wax depending on the skin and hair type, and all of our wax is hypoallergenic and formulated for sensitive skin.

Please make sure to exfoliate and moisturize your skin 48 hours before your service for the best and least painful results. After 24 hours of waxing, moisturizers and deodorants should not be applied.

Tips and FAQ

  • Brazilian: A traditional full Brazilian wax involves hair removal all around the pubic area and anus. All hair can be removed, or a triangle, square, or landing strip can be left for those of us who like a little hair down there.
  • Extended Brazilian: This is for a first-time client or anyone for whom the esthetician needs a longer time for their service. This is especially helpful if you have very dense hair, external genitals, or if it has been 3-6 months since your last wax. $90
  • Maintenance Brazilian: This is for a client who books every 4-8 weeks or if your esthetician does not need the full-time for your wax. $70
  • Bikini: This is to remove hair 2–3 fingers wide inside the bikini line, just enough to make sure there are no hairs sticking out of your bathing suit line. $40
  • Full Bikini: This service removes hair from the top and side of the bikini area but also removes all of the hair from the pubic mound. When looking at a Brazilian vs. full bikini wax, a full bikini is a good in-between for those who want all their pubic hair removed, but not to the extent of a Brazilian. $50
